This is a short English wiki for **Neo Junk City**, Steam AppID **2378230**, built as a static site for **neojunkcity.wiki**.

## Disclaimer

neojunkcity.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for *Neo Junk City*. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Starblade Games, Josh Hano, Valve, or Steam**. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. The Steam legal notice says © 2023 Josh Hano and that “Neo Junk City” is a trademark of Josh Hano. This site does not sell the game, process purchases, or recover accounts.

Nothing here is account support. The Steam store page lists http://www.nefariouslair.com/ and 16bitjosh@gmail.com. The press kit lists the same email for business and press. That site and that mailbox belong to the developer, not to this wiki.

The [guide](/guide), [walkthrough](/walkthrough), and [locations](/locations) pages quote dated Steam text. A place name appears only when the store or the press kit used it. There is no codes page because those sources do not publish a redeem program. There is no puzzle solution because those sources do not publish one.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 7 Nov 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, the demo listing, and the linked press kit were read that day. It does not mean someone finished the launch build. The full game was still marked coming soon.
</Callout>

## Images

The library hero, the library header, capsules, the store header, and screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 2378230. They were downloaded from the Steam CDN and are served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

## Privacy

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Steam login.

GA4 `G-Z2CVFHMYE5` is set in `analytics.gaId`. The base layout loads that tag on content pages.

The Adsterra Native Banner slot named `afterQuickAnswer` is enabled for neojunkcity.wiki. Zone id `b7af4332ff54544dcb0d88607e7c1b94` loads from `https://bauval.org/21/b7af4332ff54544dcb0d88607e7c1b94` on the homepage and guide pages. No advertising script from another game is included. Adult and sensitive categories stay off. The 404 page does not render a slot.

The same privacy note is in the footer of every page, under the heading Privacy. The disclaimer paragraph in that footer is the short form of this page.

## What is still unpublished

- A numeric Steam price. The 7 November appdetails payload had an empty package list, and `is_free` was false.
- A recommended spec block for the full game. Only the minimum was filled in.
- A map with district names, a chapter list for the full game, and any puzzle solution.
- Whether the 9 November build keeps the demo’s 7 February 2025 wording about the waterline and “back home.”
