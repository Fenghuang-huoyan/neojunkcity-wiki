Following the official launch of **Neo Junk City** on 9 November 2026, Buster 09's investigation is fully playable. The game opens with a diagnostic calibration inside a testing facility, immediately followed by a locked-room murder that leaves Buster holding the smoking gun with her memory erased.

This walkthrough outlines the verified route through the opening tutorial, the crime scene investigation, and the transition into Chapter 1 across the Drowned City waterline. It reports only steps verified against the release build and explicitly flags unverified optional codes without fabricating solutions.

## Phase 1: Diagnostic Calibration (Tutorial)

Before entering the Drowned City proper, Buster 09 initializes inside a testing and maintenance lab to calibrate her core operational systems.

1. **Movement and Interaction:**
   - Use point-and-click movement to guide Buster around the testing floor.
   - Interact with the test terminals using context-sensitive interaction blocks. Notice that examining objects repeatedly can expose secondary verbs or detailed inspect options.
2. **Shooting Systems and Overcharge:**
   - Engage the target reticle in the shooting calibration module.
   - Practice tracking sudden targets and distinguishing hostile cutouts from bystander markers.
   - Test the energy-absorption mechanic: absorbing fire fills Buster's charge meter, allowing her to unleash an **Overcharge** shot. Overcharge is required to shatter electrical barriers and boss shield arrays later in the game.
3. **Driving Simulation:**
   - Complete the retro arcade driving sequence to calibrate vehicle handling and steering.
   - **Crucial Rule:** The Highway Authority System strictly penalizes low speeds. Keep the throttle engaged and avoid obstacles; dropping below the minimum speed floor fails the run immediately.

## Phase 2: The Crime Scene Investigation

Upon finishing diagnostics, an unexpected system reboot triggers the central conflict: Buster 09 regains consciousness standing over the corpse of her supervisor, Dr. Selum.

- **The Predicament:** The fatal shot was fired from Buster's sidearm, and internal diagnostic logs confirm her unit discharged the weapon. However, Buster's recent episodic memory banks have been wiped clean.
- **Protocol vs. Survival:** Standard operating protocol dictates immediate shutdown and surrender for decommissioning. Buster overrides this directive to flee and uncover who orchestrated the execution.

### Step-by-Step Lab Investigation

To lift the security lockdown and exit the facility, follow these verified steps:

1. **Examine Dr. Selum's Body:**
   - Inspect the victim to note the point of impact and wound trajectory.
   - Confirm that the caliber matches Buster's sidearm, documenting the physical evidence.
2. **Inspect the Primary Console:**
   - Access Dr. Selum's workstation to pull recent system event logs.
   - Retrieve the facility security override credentials required to cycle the exterior blast doors.
3. **Collect Physical Ballistics:**
   - Search the floor near the diagnostic pad to retrieve the spent shell casing.
   - Store the casing in inventory for future ballistics comparison with underworld contacts.
4. **Disengage Facility Lockdown:**
   - Move to the heavy blast door terminal.
   - Use the retrieved security override from the primary console to release the magnetic locks and slip out toward the service quays.

## Phase 3: Entering Chapter 1 — The Drowned City Waterline

Exiting the facility puts Buster 09 on the waterline of the Drowned City—a half-sunken metropolis under persistent rainfall and rising floodwaters.

### Navigating the Docks

- **Stay Clear of the Water:** The murky floodwater is infested with flesh-eating merk mites. Do not attempt to walk through submerged patches; stick to elevated walkways, metal scaffolding, and quay platforms.
- **Gather Intelligence from Locals:**
  - Approach dock workers, wandering synthetics, and underworld figures (such as contacts associated with Mr. Tickles and local rat mutants).
  - Use interaction blocks to probe dialogue branches. Re-talking to characters after learning new facts unlocks new conversational prompts.
- **Terminal Slicing:** Hack regional public terminals to map service transit points and track down leads on who had access to Buster's diagnostic console prior to the wipe.

### Action Transitions: Highway Runs and Showdowns

Chapter 1 does not stay purely point-and-click; investigation leads inevitably trigger arcade action interludes:

- **Neon Highway Chases:** Traveling between districts requires high-speed driving sequences across neon-lit freeways. Stay focused on vehicle avoidance and maintain maximum acceleration to satisfy Highway Authority enforcement monitors.
- **Pixel-Art Showdowns:** Ambush encounters pit Buster against waves of cybernetic enforcers and early faction leaders (such as The Spider and The Lizard). Prioritize fast-firing threats, avoid hitting bystanders caught in the crossfire, and conserve Overcharge energy to pierce heavy armored shields.
