## Phase 3: Entering Chapter 1 — The Drowned City Waterline

Exiting the facility puts Buster 09 on the waterline of the Drowned City—a half-sunken metropolis under persistent rainfall and rising floodwaters.

### Navigating the Docks

- **Stay Clear of the Water:** The murky floodwater is described as infested with flesh-eating mites (verified by Steam store). Do not attempt to walk through submerged water patches.
- **Gather Intelligence from Locals (Unverified):**
  - Player accounts report exploring dock walkways and speaking with local synthetics and underworld contacts (including references to Mr. Tickles and local rat mutants, noted in store teasers).

Chapter 1 alternates between point-and-click investigation and arcade action interludes:

- **Neon Highway Chases:** Traveling across districts features high-speed driving sequences across neon-lit freeways. Maintain speed to avoid Highway Authority penalties (verified by Steam store).
