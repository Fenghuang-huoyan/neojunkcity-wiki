This is the overview for **Neo Junk City** before the 9 November 2026 unlock. ## What Steam says the game is

The short description on the store:

> Sinister tides are rising in the Drowned City. Dive into a Point and Click action-RPG with fast-paced shoot-outs and retro arcade racing. Uncover the truth behind a murder you don't remember committing!

The longer text sets the game in a dystopian half-sunken metropolis it calls the Drowned City. You investigate murders, race through streets, and shoot through “hordes of robots, psychopaths, and cybernetic monsters.” The closing line asks whether you can save the city from itself as the water keeps rising.

## Buster 09

The store’s story bullet: as Buster 09, you navigate the Drowned City to solve a murder. “The only catch? You already know you did it. You just can't remember why.”

The press kit is more specific and uses different wording. It calls Buster 09 a law-enforcement android, says she is the murderer, and says the memory was wiped. This page reports both sentences. It does not merge them into a scene-by-scene plot.

Store screenshots include a combat bar labeled BUSTER. That is text in the image. The store page does not attach a biography to the screenshot.

## Investigate, drive, shoot

The store and the press kit use the same three verbs. The store gives each one a paragraph.

**Investigate.** Context-sensitive “interaction blocks” change as you explore and as relationships change. The store says interaction icons evolve, hidden interactions open, and new paths and bonds appear. It does not list the icon set or a puzzle recipe.

**Drive.** Racing is described as fast, retro, and arcade-like, on neon-lit streets. The **Highway Authority System** “strictly monitors those on the road.” The store’s own warning: drive too slowly and they will not only revoke your license, they will revoke your right to live. No lap times, car list, or track names are printed.

**Shoot.** Targets appear quickly. The store says you distinguish threats from bystanders and that timing decides the fight. It calls the mode a pixel-art showdown in the style of retro arcade games. No weapon stats are printed.

A separate bullet says the world changes as your impact on the community is felt. No example of a changed location is given.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Two Steam lines about timing">
The category list includes “Playable without Timed Input.” The same store page describes sudden targets and a penalty for driving too slowly. Both are on the page. This guide does not guess which one a given scene follows.
</Callout>

## The free demo

The full-game page says a free demo of the first chapter is live. The demo’s own app, **2474130**, has `is_free: true`, `type: demo`, and a release date of 7 Feb 2025 with `coming_soon: false`. Its description:

> Dive into the first chapter of Neo Junk City. Get a feel for the world and explore the waterline of the drowned city as Buster 09. Investigate, drive, and shoot your way through the dregs and try to find your way back home, while trying to solve a murder you have no memory of committing.

The full-game page adds a looser teaser: mite-infested waters, a giant mutant rat lady, a holographic car in a synthetic universe, and the name “mr.tickles.” Those are store sentences, not a room list. They are repeated, and limited, on the [walkthrough](/walkthrough) page.

Demo minimum specs are not the full game’s specs. Steam lists Windows 10, a 64-bit CPU, Intel Core2 Duo E6400 at 2.13 GHz, 8 GB RAM, GeForce GT 730 or Radeon HD 4830, DirectX 11, and 1 GB of space. The recommended block on the demo payload only restates a 64-bit processor and operating system. The full game asks for 5 GB and a newer CPU and GPU, and it did not publish a recommended block.

The demo supports the same eight languages as the full game’s language string: English, Portuguese - Brazil, German, Spanish - Latin America, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Russian.

## Languages, content notes, and what is missing

On the English store page, all eight languages have a check for Interface and a check for Subtitles. Full Audio is unchecked for English and for the other seven. This wiki is English only.

The developer content note says Neo Junk City includes mild sexual jokes, adult language, mature themes, and humor that may not suit every player. `required_age` in appdetails is 0. The ratings object included DEJUS 12, Steam Germany 12, and IGRS 18. No ESRB rating was in that payload.
