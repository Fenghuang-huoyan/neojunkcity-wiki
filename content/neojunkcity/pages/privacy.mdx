This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to **neojunkcity.wiki**, an independent fan-made guide for *Neo Junk City*. It is not Starblade Games’, Josh Hano’s, or Valve Corporation’s privacy policy.

## 1. Static Site & Zero Account Data

The site is served as static HTML and CSS files. It does not offer user accounts, comment forms, or checkout pages, and never asks for your Steam credentials or personal information.

## 2. Analytics & Cookies (`GA4`)

`G-Z2CVFHMYE5` is configured in `sites/neojunkcity/site.config.ts` and loaded via `googletagmanager.com/gtag/js`. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_*`) in your browser to distinguish anonymous sessions and measure aggregated page views. You can block cookies in your browser settings or install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 3. Advertising Status

Display advertising in `sites/neojunkcity/ads.config.ts` is **disabled** (`enabled: false`). No external banner or third-party ad network scripts execute on the homepage, guide pages, or 404 page.

For editorial corrections or privacy inquiries regarding **neojunkcity.wiki**, email `malee05050264@gmail.com`. (The developer support address printed on Steam belongs to Starblade Games and is not read by this fan wiki.)
